# Level 9 — Cloud-Native & Platform — Exercises

Practice problems keyed to the [09-cloud-platform](../../docs/09-cloud-platform/README.md) level.

## Estimation & reasoning drills

- 1. Design a canary + feature-flag rollout that aborts automatically on error-budget burn.
- 2. Autoscaling app replicas while the DB is the bottleneck — why doesn't it help, and what does?
- 3. Map zero-downtime deploys to graceful shutdown + readiness probes + rolling strategy.

## Design prompts

- 4. When is a service mesh worth its sidecar overhead, and when not?
- 5. Make GitOps pull-based and explain why it's more auditable than push-based deploys.

## What would break? / when NOT to use

- 6. Design golden paths on an IDP that bake in observability + mTLS by default.

> Answers are intentionally open-ended; discuss trade-offs and constraints. See the matching chapters and the [interview framework](../../interview-framework/README.md).
