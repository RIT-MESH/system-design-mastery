# Level 8 — Observability & Operations — Exercises

Practice problems keyed to the [08-observability](../../docs/08-observability/README.md) level.

## Estimation & reasoning drills

- 1. Compose logs+metrics+traces to diagnose a slow multi-hop checkout (which signal answers what?).
- 2. A metric labeled by user_id explodes cost. Move the detail to the right signal.
- 3. Design a multi-window burn-rate alert for a 99.9% SLO.

## Design prompts

- 4. A runbook says 'investigate.' Rewrite it to be useful at 3am.
- 5. Mitigate-first vs diagnose-first: why mitigate first, and what evidence must you capture first?

## What would break? / when NOT to use

- 6. Add synthetic + RUM for a checkout flow; what does each catch that the other misses?

> Answers are intentionally open-ended; discuss trade-offs and constraints. See the matching chapters and the [interview framework](../../interview-framework/README.md).
