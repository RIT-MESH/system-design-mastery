# Architecture Decision Record (ADR) Template

> An ADR captures a single significant architectural decision, its context, consequences,
> and status. Keep one decision per file. Store under `templates/adr/` or co-locate with the
> system. Adapted loosely from the Michael Nygard ADR pattern; this template is original.
